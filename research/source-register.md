# Wissa Sobhy Archive — Source Register

## Purpose

This register separates direct evidence about Father Wissa Sobhy Tadros from contextual evidence about the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga, its bishops, churches, clergy, and historical setting.

## Identity and ministry — verified / high confidence

- Full ecclesiastical name: Father Wissa Sobhy Tadros.
- Ordination date: 11 February 1991.
- Priesthood rank elevation (hegomen/archpriest title as reported): 22 June 1997.
- Service: Saint George Coptic Orthodox Church, Deir Mawas, Minya.
- Role reported by St-Takla: Deputy/Vicar of the Diocese of Deir Mawas.
- St-Takla source: https://st-takla.org/characters/priest-pastor-father/waaw/weeca-subhi-tadrus.html
- Saint George Church source: https://st-takla.org/Coptic-History/places/africa/egypt/deir-mwas-delga/saint-george-church.html

## 2026 milestone source — high confidence

On 8 April 2026, El Balad reported that Bishop Pakhom of Deir Mawas and Delga and the diocesan clergy commemorated 35 years since Father Wissa Sobhy's ordination. The article also reports that he grew up in Tanta, obtained a Bachelor of Commerce degree in 1980, served at Saint Michael Church in Tanta before ordination, and was ordained by Bishop Agabius of Deir Mawas and Bishop Thomas of Qusiya. Treat these details as source-backed claims, pending comparison with church/family records.

Source: https://www.elbalad.news/6928700

## 2013 direct eyewitness/reporting source — high-value primary media evidence

Wataninet published a detailed September 2013 field report from Deir Mawas that accompanied Father Wissa Sobhy, identified as Deputy of the Diocese of Deir Mawas, to Saint Lazarus Church, the adjacent cemetery, the diocesan service building, and streets of Deir Mawas after the August 2013 attacks. The report records his description of the church's damage and his identification of damage to his own home and diocesan property.

Source: https://www.wataninet.com/2013/09/%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%B1%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B3-%D8%A8%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7/

## 2013 heritage source — direct quotation

RAWI Publishing's article "Burning History" identifies Father Wissa Sobhy of the bishopric of Deir Mawas and quotes him explaining the Church's priority of preserving its flock over preserving buildings. The article discusses historic churches damaged in the August 2013 violence, including the ancient Virgin Mary and Father Abram Church in Deir Mawas and the Virgin Mary church in Delga.

Source: https://rawi-publishing.com/articles/burning-history?lang=en

## 2007 historical event lead — needs independent corroboration

A CopticHistory.org page reports that a group of Deir Mawas clergy were detained at the police station on 24 April 2007 and explicitly lists Father Wissa Sobhy among them. Because this is a secondary advocacy/history site, preserve it as a lead and do not mark the event as independently verified until corroborated by contemporary press, court/government records, or another independent source.

Source: https://www.coptichistory.org/new_page_2405.htm

## Birth date and secular name — caution

A low-authority mirror/aggregator page currently states that Father Wissa Sobhy's secular name is Samir Sobhy and gives a birth date of 4 December 1956 in Tanta. The secular name is independently echoed by the 2026 El Balad article, which says the deacon "Samir Sobhy" became Father Wissa Sobhy at ordination. The 1956 birth date has not yet been confirmed by a strong source and must remain UNVERIFIED.
