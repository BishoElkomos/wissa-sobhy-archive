# Research Delta — 27 August 2026

## New evidence and research decisions

### 2010 — source-chain reconstruction
The preserved Coptic History page for the Camelia Shehata episode contains material attributed to multiple contemporary publications, including Al-Wafd, Al-Quds, Al-Youm Al-Sabea and Rose al-Youssef. Treat this as a discovery map, not as multiple independent sources. Individual original articles should be recovered and catalogued separately.

Useful source trail:
- https://coptichistory.org/untitled_1227.htm
- https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/14080

The page also preserves a passage attributed to Al-Youm Al-Sabea in which Wissa Sobhy is quoted about the protest and police handling. This is valuable for locating the original newspaper item.
