# Citation & Research Guide

## Purpose
Make every important historical claim traceable and make the archive useful for students, journalists, clergy, historians and researchers.

## Recommended citation fields
For a web source:
- Author.
- Article/page title.
- Publisher.
- Publication date.
- URL.
- Access date when useful.
- Archived URL when available.

For a photograph:
- Photographer/creator if known.
- Caption/title.
- Date.
- Event/place.
- Collection or publisher.
- Original URL or document reference.
- Rights/license.

For video/audio:
- Creator/channel/broadcaster.
- Title.
- Publication date.
- Platform.
- URL.
- Timestamp for the cited passage when available.
- Transcript/translation reference.

## Citation levels
- **Primary source** — original document, recording, photograph, official record or direct testimony.
- **Contemporaneous source** — reporting created close to the event.
- **Independent corroboration** — separate source confirming a material fact without simply copying the same report.
- **Secondary source** — later interpretation or compilation.
- **Archival witness** — preserved copy useful for provenance even if the original publisher is unavailable.
- **Family testimony** — information supplied by family members; valuable but explicitly labeled.

## Quotations
Use quotations accurately and minimally. Preserve the original language for significant quotations and provide a reviewed translation where needed. Do not merge separate statements into a single quotation.

## Citation of the archive itself
Researchers may cite a page using:
**Archive name — page title — collection/entity — URL — access date.**

Where a page contains a documented claim, researchers are encouraged to cite the underlying primary or contemporaneous source as well as the archive page.

## Academic integrity
The archive may be used for research, but researchers remain responsible for independently evaluating sources and citing them accurately. The archive is not a substitute for examining primary documents where those are available.
