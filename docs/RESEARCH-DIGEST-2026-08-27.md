## High-confidence / strong published evidence

### Father Wissa Sobhy
- St-Takla identifies Father Wissa Sobhy Tadrous, service at St George Church in Deir Mawas, ordination 11 Feb 1991, priesthood rank elevated to Archpriest 22 Jun 1997, and role as deputy of the diocese. Source: https://st-takla.org/characters/priest-pastor-father/waaw/weeca-subhi-tadrus.html
- April 8, 2026 reporting on the 35th anniversary of his ordination states that he grew up in Tanta, earned a Bachelor of Commerce in 1980, served at St Michael Church in Tanta before ordination, was ordained by Anba Agabius and Anba Thomas, spent forty days at the Monastery of the Virgin Mary in Muharraq, and began pastoral service on 1 Mar 1991. It also attributes several administrative and construction projects to his service, including the marriage/documentation office, rebuilding St George Cathedral in Deir Mawas, churches in Muhram, Nazlet El-Badraman, Rahmania and Tukh, expansion of the diocesan church, diocesan headquarters, Delga monastery wall, cemetery wall, Lazarus the Bishop, Nicodemus/Joseph of Arimathea, and Abu Seifein foundation. Treat project dates/details as claims requiring source-by-source verification. Source: https://www.elbalad.news/6928700
- A 2017 YouTube upload of the ordination liturgy explicitly describes the ordination of Father Wissa Sobhy by Anba Agabius and Anba Thomas at the Virgin Mary Church of the Deir Mawas diocese. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kgdkaV-C3jo

### 2007 Deir Mawas
- Al-Masry Al-Youm, 29 Apr 2007, names Father Wissa Sobhy among diocesan priests present during execution of a possession/enforcement order concerning a property on Union Street and reports a clash involving the metropolitan's side. This is a strong contemporaneous source for his presence in the event, but individual allegations within the article should not be repeated without attribution. Source: https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/2138588
- CopticHistory and old forum mirrors report that 14 priests, including Father Wissa Sobhy, were detained at Deir Mawas police station on 24 Apr 2007. These are archival/secondary reports; retain as leads and corroborate with contemporaneous independent reporting before publishing as settled fact. Sources: https://www.coptichistory.org/new_page_2405.htm ; https://arabchurch.com/forums/threads/ذاكرة-الاقباط-كى-لا-ننسى.116907/page-2

## Anba Agabius / diocesan history
- Official Coptic Orthodox Church page records Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga founded 13 Nov 1988 with Anba Agabius, and after his repose on 4 Feb 2025 Anba Boktor was ordained bishop on 1 Jun 2025. Source: https://copticorthodox.church/dioceses/der-mouas/
- Official church biography records Anba Agabius: born 2 Apr 1944, monasticism 15 Dec 1977, episcopal ordination 13 Nov 1988, metropolitan promotion 20 Nov 2022, repose 4 Feb 2025. Source: https://copticorthodox.church/past-bishops/الأنبا-أغابيوس/
- Contemporary 2025 reporting gives additional pre-episcopal context: service in Tanta's tribal districts at the Virgin Mary and Archangel Michael churches; monasticism at St Bishoy; ordained priest 17 Jul 1979 as the monk Wissa of St Bishoy; elevated to hegumen 1 Jan 1981 and appointed steward of St Bishoy Monastery; papal assignments in 1985. Sources: https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_regions/details/2025/2/4/2721535/ ; https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/7820249
- St-Takla explicitly identifies the monastic name Wissa of St Bishoy as Anba Agabius and records his appointment as steward of St Bishoy Monastery on 1 Jan 1981. Source: https://st-takla.org/Coptic-History/places/monasteries/africa/egypt/st-bishoy/trustees.html
- This identity must be kept separate from Father Wissa Sobhy Tadrous in the data model. Name similarity is not identity.
- Pope Tawadros' funeral remarks, reported by Masrawy, describe Anba Agabius as steward of St Bishoy from 1981–1985 and characterize his Deir Mawas service as a long pastoral legacy. Source: https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2025/2/5/2722175/

## Research cautions
1. Do not merge Anba Agabius's monastic name "Wissa" with Father Wissa Sobhy.
2. Do not infer the family-reported friendships/mentorships from mere co-occurrence. Current family claims: Father Isaac Yacoub, William Kyrillos, Father Boutros Eida as lifelong friends; Father Barsoum Henein as spiritual mentor.
3. Do not treat repeated syndication of the 2026 anniversary article as multiple independent sources.
4. Treat 2007 detention claims from CopticHistory/forums as leads requiring independent corroboration.
5. Treat project/construction claims from the 2026 anniversary article as claims to be broken into individual project records and verified with church records, photos, cornerstone/dedication reports, or dated sources.
6. Family-supplied birth date 4 Dec 1956 is recorded as family-confirmed; web corroboration exists in a secondary source, but an original civil/church record is still preferred.
