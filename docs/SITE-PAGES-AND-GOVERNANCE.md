# Public Website Pages & Governance

## Purpose
Define the public-facing pages required for a trustworthy, bilingual historical archive and the governance layer behind them.

## Core public pages
- **من نحن / About the Archive** — mission, scope, methodology, history of the project, editorial principles, and future expansion.
- **تواصل معنا / Contact** — structured contact routes for corrections, source contributions, media submissions, rights claims, and general inquiries.
- **كيف نوثق؟ / How We Document** — explanation of source grading, corroboration, provenance, uncertainty, and archival workflow.
- **المصادر / Sources** — searchable source directory with publisher, date, language, source type, provenance and evidence grade.
- **الخط الزمني / Timeline** — chronological navigation through people, events, places and sources.
- **الأشخاص / People** — connected profiles for clergy, family, mentors, friends, officials, journalists and other historical figures.
- **الأماكن / Places** — Deir Mawas, Delga, churches, diocesan buildings and related locations.
- **الأحداث / Events** — event-centered historical records with evidence trails.
- **المقالات والدراسات / Articles & Studies** — long-form historical writing and source-critical essays.
- **المعرض / Gallery** — photographs and scanned visual material with provenance and rights labels.
- **الفيديو والصوت / Video & Audio** — audiovisual archive with transcripts and source metadata when available.
- **الوثائق / Documents** — family, ecclesiastical, press and archival documents where publication is authorized.
- **ساهم في التوثيق / Contribute** — invite public submissions while requiring provenance and permission.
- **الأسئلة الشائعة / FAQ** — explain the archive, sourcing, corrections, citations, privacy and media rights.

## Required legal and trust pages
- **شروط الاستخدام / Terms of Use**
- **حقوق النشر والملكية الفكرية / Copyright & Intellectual Property**
- **سياسة الاقتباس والإسناد / Citation & Attribution Policy**
- **سياسة الخصوصية / Privacy Policy**
- **سياسة ملفات الارتباط / Cookie Policy** when cookies or similar technologies are actually used.
- **سياسة التصحيح / Corrections Policy**
- **سياسة طلب الإزالة والنزاعات على الحقوق / Takedown & Rights Claims**
- **إخلاء المسؤولية / Disclaimer**
- **سياسة المساهمات / Contributions Policy**
- **إمكانية الوصول / Accessibility Statement**

## Contact workflow
The contact page should separate:
1. Historical correction.
2. Source/document submission.
3. Photograph/video/audio submission.
4. Copyright or rights claim.
5. Privacy or personal-data request.
6. Research collaboration.
7. General inquiry.

Each route should request only information necessary to process the request and should avoid collecting sensitive personal data through an unprotected public form.

## Editorial governance
The archive should maintain an internal record of:
- source review;
- claim verification;
- corrections;
- disputed identifications;
- takedown requests;
- rights permissions;
- publication decisions;
- translation review.

## Transparency
The About page should explain that the archive is a historical documentation project. It should distinguish:
- family-provided material;
- ecclesiastical records;
- contemporaneous journalism;
- independent corroboration;
- archival copies;
- oral testimony;
- unresolved claims.

## Accessibility
The public website should target WCAG 2.2 AA as a practical design goal, including keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, alt text for meaningful images, captions/transcripts for audiovisual material where feasible, visible focus states, semantic headings, and language metadata.

## Search-engine integrity
The website should use descriptive titles, canonical URLs, hreflang, structured data, XML sitemap, robots directives, Open Graph metadata and clean internal linking. Search optimization must never override historical accuracy.

## Versioning and auditability
Important editorial records should remain versioned. Corrections should not erase the audit trail. Public pages may display a concise revision note while detailed editorial history remains internal unless disclosure is appropriate.
