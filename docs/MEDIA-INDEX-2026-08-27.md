## Media preservation policy
1. Do not hotlink critical archival assets as the only preservation layer.
2. Record original URL, source, publication date, uploader/page identity, filename, media type, and verification status.
3. Preserve thumbnails/screenshots only when copyright or technical constraints prevent lawful archival copying; retain the source URL for attribution.
4. Never crop out captions, watermarks, dates, or contextual metadata from archival master copies.
5. Create separate records for the same photograph appearing in different publications; link them as duplicates/derivatives rather than deleting one.
6. Audio, video, photo galleries, PDFs, scanned documents, livestreams and social posts all receive first-class media records.
7. For social media, record platform, public page/profile, post URL, post date, media count, and whether the page identity is verified or merely inferred.
8. Do not expose private contact information or private social-media content.
