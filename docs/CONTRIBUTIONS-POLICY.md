# Contributions Policy

## Purpose
The archive welcomes historically useful contributions while protecting provenance, privacy, copyright and editorial integrity.

## What may be contributed
- Family photographs and documents.
- Church or diocesan historical material.
- Personal photographs and recordings.
- Newspaper clippings and archival references.
- Oral-history testimony.
- Corrections and additional context.
- Links to public photographs, videos, audio and documents.

## Contributor declaration
A contributor should state, to the best of their knowledge:
- who created or owns the material;
- how they obtained it;
- approximate date and place;
- people/events depicted;
- whether they have permission to submit it;
- any restrictions or requested attribution.

Submitting a link is not the same as granting a license to reproduce the material.

## Editorial review
All contributions are reviewed before publication. Acceptance does not mean that every claim is independently verified. The archive may label material as family testimony, oral history, archival lead, or independently corroborated evidence.

## Permissions
Where the archive needs a license to publish a supplied work, the permission should specify scope, media, languages, duration and whether the use is exclusive or non-exclusive. Unless explicitly agreed, no contributor transfers ownership of copyright merely by submitting material.

## Privacy and sensitive material
Contributors should not submit sensitive personal information about living persons unless there is a legitimate archival reason and an appropriate basis or permission. The archive may redact or decline publication.

## Corrections
Contributors may submit corrections with evidence. Corrections are reviewed under the archive's Corrections Policy and do not automatically replace a documented historical account.

## Attribution
When a contribution is published, the archive will provide attribution according to the contributor's requested form where appropriate and compatible with the applicable rights.
