# Deep Research Update — 2026-08-27

## Scope

Web research was expanded across Father Wissa Sobhy, Deir Mawas/Delga, Bishop Boktor, Nader Shokry, 2007, 2010, 2013, 2025 and 2026. Results are research evidence, not automatically publication-ready claims.

## Father Wissa Sobhy — high-value findings

1. St-Takla identifies Father Wissa Sobhy Tadros, records ordination on 11 February 1991, hegumen rank on 22 June 1997, service at St George Church in Deir Mawas, and his role as diocesan deputy. Use as a church/reference source; do not treat it as independent corroboration by itself.
2. Al-Masry Al-Youm (29 April 2007) names Father Wissa among the Deir Mawas clergy present during a dispute over a property acquired for the diocese. Preserve this as a contemporary event record and distinguish allegations from established facts.
3. A 2007 forum/secondary archive independently preserves a report dated 24 April 2007 naming Father Wissa among clergy detained at the Deir Mawas police station. This is a lead for archival recovery, not a final high-confidence source.
4. Copts United (23 July 2010) records Father Wissa, identified as diocesan deputy, speaking during the public demonstrations concerning the disappearance of a priest's wife. This is a direct contemporary attribution and should become a source-linked claim.
5. Al-Masry Al-Youm coverage in July 2010 also records a statement attributed to Father Wissa concerning a recording and the location of the woman involved. Preserve the exact attribution and context; do not infer facts beyond the report.
6. Mobtada (15 September 2013) records a person identified as Wissa Sobhy describing conditions in Delga and the attacks on churches, including St George and the Virgin/Anba Abraam churches. Verify identity and role against the Wataninet report and other contemporaneous sources.
7. Wataninet's September 2013 field report explicitly says its team toured Deir Mawas with Father Wissa Sobhy, identified as deputy of the diocese, documenting damage to churches, cemeteries and the diocesan service building. This is one of the strongest contemporaneous sources for his public role during the 2013 crisis.
8. RAWI Publishing's Burning History includes an attributed statement by Father Wissa Sobhi about the Church prioritizing preservation of its flock's souls over preserving brick and mortar. Preserve the article as an English-language heritage source and link it to the 2013 church-destruction context.
9. Sada El-Balad (8 April 2026) reports the diocese's commemoration of 35 years since Father Wissa's ordination and attributes background information to the occasion, including Tanta upbringing, commerce degree in 1980, service at St Michael in Tanta, and ordination by Anba Agabius and Anba Thomas. Treat these biographical details as supported by the event report but seek primary/independent confirmation before labeling them fully verified.
10. A YouTube upload of the ordination liturgy exists, published in 2017. The upload date is not the event date. Its description identifies the ordination as performed by Anba Agabius and Anba Thomas at the Virgin Mary Church in Deir Mawas. Preserve as audiovisual evidence and verify the original recording/date if possible.

## Nader Shokry connection

Nader Shokry is now a source-side entity of interest, not automatically a personal associate of Father Wissa. Evidence shows:
- He authored the Wataninet 2013 Deir Mawas field report involving Father Wissa.
- He authored multiple Copts United reports concerning Deir Mawas/Bishop Boktor in 2025–2026.
- Copts United and Wataninet results establish repeated journalistic coverage of the same diocesan ecosystem, but do not by themselves prove a personal relationship. Any relationship should be sourced separately.

The 2025 enthronement coverage explicitly mentions representatives of Al-Azhar and Awqaf at the ceremony. Separate 2 July 2025 reporting documents the Awqaf leadership's congratulatory visit and Bishop Boktor's remarks about relations among Al-Azhar, Awqaf and the Egyptian Church. The project should create an institutional relationship/event record rather than reducing this to a generic biography sentence.

## Critical corrections / cautions

- Do not label 11 February 1991 as a birth date; it is the ordination date.
- A secondary page currently states a birth date of 4 December 1956; this remains unverified and must not be treated as established until supported by stronger evidence.
- Do not infer the exact nature or duration of Father Wissa's relationship with Nader Shokry from repeated coverage.
- Do not merge Anba Boktor with the similarly named Bishop Boktor of the New Valley from older articles; identity disambiguation is mandatory.
- Do not treat a forum post as equivalent to contemporary newspaper or primary evidence.
- News reports containing accusations must remain attributed to the source/speaker.
