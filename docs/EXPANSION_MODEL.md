# Expansion Model

The first public collection centers on Father Wissa Sobhy. The architecture must also support future collections without a migration of the conceptual model.

## Future collection layers

### Person collections
- Father Wissa Sobhy Tadros
- Anba Agabius
- clergy and church servants
- local historians and journalists
- other historically relevant people

### Geographic collections
- Deir Mawas
- Delga
- villages and surrounding localities
- churches and monasteries
- diocesan institutions

### Institutional collection
- Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga
- churches
- schools/services
- media and civic organizations

### Historical themes
- church history
- local social history
- major events
- restoration/rebuilding
- clergy history
- press history

## Collection model

A future collection is a curated view over shared entities. Avoid duplicating a person, place, or event simply because it appears in more than one collection.

## Governance

Each collection should have its own editorial scope, source policy, publication status, and optional curator metadata while sharing the common archive engine.
