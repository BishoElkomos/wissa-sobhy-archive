# Archive Collections

The public archive will be organized into collections that share one underlying knowledge graph.

## Foundational collections

### 01 — Wissa Sobhy Archive
The initial collection: biography, formation, ordination, ministry, testimony, media, documents, press coverage, and relationships.

### 02 — Anba Agabius Archive
Biography, episcopal ministry, Deir Mawas and Delga, clergy, churches, events, writings, photographs, and relationship network.

### 03 — Anba Bakhter Archive
The contemporary episcopal period beginning with the 2025 episcopal consecration and enthronement, followed by documented diocesan activities and developments.

### 04 — Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga
Institutional history, episcopal succession, clergy, churches, services, geography, boundaries, major events, and sources.

### 05 — Churches and Heritage
Individual church histories, architecture, artifacts, icons, manuscripts, clergy, events, images, and restoration history.

### 06 — Deir Mawas & Dalga Local History
Local geography, communities, major historical events, social history, press history, and documented heritage.

### 07 — Events Archive
Cross-collection chronological records with people, places, institutions, evidence, and media.

### 08 — Press & Publications
Newspaper articles, interviews, reports, books, websites, archived pages, and journalist/source metadata.

### 09 — Photographic & Document Archive
Curated archival media with provenance, rights, descriptions, OCR where applicable, and links to related records.

## Collection rules

Collections are curated views over shared entities. Do not duplicate the same person, place, church, or event solely because it appears in multiple collections.

A collection page should expose context and relationships so visitors can move naturally from one historical record to another.
