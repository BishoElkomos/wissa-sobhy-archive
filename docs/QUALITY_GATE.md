# Archive Quality Gate

This project is a historical archive. No content is considered publication-ready merely because it appears online.

## Before publishing a factual claim

1. Identify the exact claim and date.
2. Prefer the original publication or primary document.
3. Record title, author, publisher, publication date, URL, and access date.
4. Compare independent sources when the claim is significant or disputed.
5. Separate source wording from our editorial interpretation.
6. Mark uncertainty explicitly: `verified`, `corroborated`, `needs-review`, or `unverified`.
7. Preserve the source even if a later source contradicts it.

## Media

Every photo/document/video should have provenance where known: date, place, event, people, creator/source, rights status, and notes. Do not invent dates or identities from appearance alone.

## Privacy and safety

Do not publish private contact details, sensitive personal data, credentials, or unpublished material without appropriate authorization.

## Editorial principle

The archive documents history; it does not manufacture certainty. Conflicting accounts should remain distinguishable and linked to their sources.
