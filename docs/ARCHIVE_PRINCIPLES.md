# Archive Principles

## Purpose

Create a durable, elegant, searchable digital record of Father Wissa Sobhy Tadros and the historical context of his ministry.

## The archive is a knowledge graph

A person can connect to events, places, churches, publications, photographs, documents, and sources. Records should be reusable across pages rather than copied into isolated articles.

## Source hierarchy

1. Primary documents and contemporaneous records
2. Independent reputable journalism
3. Institutional/church publications
4. Established reference works
5. Archived copies and secondary reproductions
6. User/family recollections — valuable, but explicitly identified as such

## Design philosophy

Quiet, dignified, editorial, modern, and image-led. The visual language should suggest Egyptian/Coptic heritage without becoming ornamental or distracting. Readability, accessibility, performance, and mobile experience are first-class requirements.

## Long-term goal

The system should support decades of additions without requiring a redesign: new events, sources, photographs, documents, interviews, corrections, and translations should fit the same underlying model.
