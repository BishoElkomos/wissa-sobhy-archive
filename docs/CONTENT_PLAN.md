## Record page pattern

Every public record should have:

- clear title
- date/place context
- concise editorial summary
- related people/places/events
- source panel
- confidence/provenance where useful
- related archive items
- accessible image captions and alt text

## Editorial tone

Dignified, factual, warm, restrained. Avoid promotional superlatives unless directly attributed to a source. Preserve the distinction between family testimony, institutional accounts, journalism, and primary documents.
