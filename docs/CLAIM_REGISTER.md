## Rules

- `corroborated` means multiple suitable pieces of evidence support the claim; it does not mean every detail is independently verified.
- A source can support context without proving a direct relationship.
- Allegations in news reporting must remain attributed to the reporting source or named speaker.
- Candidate claims should be migrated into the structured Claim model only after their provenance fields are complete.
- This register is not the public biography.
