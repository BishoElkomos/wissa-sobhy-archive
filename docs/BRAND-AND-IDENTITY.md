# Brand & Identity Charter

## Canonical identity
The public archive is named after its principal historical subject:

**القمص ويصا صبحي تادرس**

English:

**Archpriest Wissa Sobhy Tadrous**

The archive's identity must never obscure this central subject. The wider historical collections are an expansion of the archive, not a replacement for its principal identity.

## Positioning
The project is a dedicated digital historical archive documenting the life, priestly ministry, relationships, service, testimony, historical context, photographs, audiovisual record and documentary footprint of Archpriest Wissa Sobhy Tadrous, while preserving a carefully designed path toward the wider history of Deir Mawas, the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga, its clergy, churches, institutions and related historical figures.

## Site naming
The final public domain/brand should prominently contain the name **Wissa Sobhy** in a form that is natural for Arabic and English audiences. The GitHub repository name is an implementation detail and does not define the final public brand.

## Homepage hierarchy
The homepage should make the principal subject immediately clear:
1. Name and identity of Archpriest Wissa Sobhy Tadrous.
2. Short archival mission statement.
3. Featured biography / life chronology.
4. Major historical milestones.
5. People and mentors connected to his life.
6. Events he witnessed, participated in or documented.
7. Curated photographs and audiovisual material.
8. Historical articles and source collections.
9. Wider Deir Mawas / diocesan history as an explicitly related expansion.

## Relationship to future collections
Future collections must appear as connected branches:
- Wissa Sobhy Archive — principal collection.
- Anba Agabius — formative ecclesiastical and historical context.
- Anba Boktor — contemporary diocesan context.
- Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga.
- Deir Mawas and Delga local history.
- Churches and institutions.

These collections should never make the site appear to have changed its principal subject. The site's visual and navigational hierarchy should always allow a visitor to return to the Wissa Sobhy archive in one step.

## Editorial character
The visual and written identity should be dignified, restrained and archival rather than commercial. It may express the Coptic Orthodox cultural and ecclesiastical context through typography, iconography and respectful language without presenting historical research as official church pronouncement unless a source is genuinely official.

## Attribution
The site should clearly distinguish:
- archive editorial content;
- family-provided material;
- church/institutional sources;
- press sources;
- public social-media material;
- third-party photographs/video/audio;
- scholarly interpretation.

## Consistency
Use the same canonical person ID and identity across Arabic and English. Titles must be historically appropriate to the date of the event. Do not retroactively alter historical roles for visual convenience.
