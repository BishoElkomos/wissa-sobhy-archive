# Editorial & Terminology Style Guide

## Scope
This guide governs Arabic editorial language across the Wissa Sobhy Archive and its future expansion into Deir Mawas history, the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga, Anba Agabius, Anba Boktor, churches, clergy, people, places, events, media and documentary sources.

## Editorial voice
The archive should read as:
- historically rigorous;
- ecclesiastically literate and consistent with Coptic Orthodox usage;
- respectful without devotional exaggeration in historical descriptions;
- precise about titles, dates, places and ecclesiastical offices;
- transparent about source provenance and uncertainty;
- readable to both specialists and the general public.

## Core terminology
Prefer established Coptic Orthodox and historical terms where the source and context support them:
- **نيافة الأنبا** for a bishop in formal ecclesiastical context.
- **نيافة الحبر الجليل** only where a formal commemorative or ecclesiastical style calls for it; avoid repetitive honorifics in database fields.
- **القمص** for an archpriest; preserve the title as historically used by the source.
- **أبونا / الأب الكاهن** in narrative or oral-history contexts when appropriate; use **الكاهن** in neutral metadata.
- **القمص ويصا صبحي تادرس** as the canonical Arabic name unless a source uses another form.
- **Father Wissa Sobhy / Wissa Sobhy Tadrous / Wisa Soubhy Tadrous** as source-preserving transliterations when relevant.
- **نياحة** for the repose/death of a clergyman or Christian figure in ecclesiastical narrative; use **وفاة** in neutral administrative metadata where appropriate.
- **تجليس** for the formal enthronement/seating of a bishop, not generic appointment, when that is what the event was.
- **سيامة كاهن** for priestly ordination.
- **القمصية / منح رتبة القمصية** for elevation to archpriesthood where historically accurate.
- **الإيبارشية** for diocese in Coptic Orthodox usage.
- **المطرانية** where the institution is specifically a metropolitanate/bishopric seat as historically named; do not force terminology where the source uses another official name.
- **الكنيسة القبطية الأرثوذكسية** as the formal church name.
- **خدمة رعوية** for pastoral ministry.
- **رعاية شعب الإيبارشية** where the source supports pastoral context.
- **تدشين** for church consecration/dedication events; do not substitute generic inauguration when the ecclesiastical event was a consecration.
- **قداس إلهي** for the Divine Liturgy in ecclesiastical narrative.
- **صلاة عشية / صلاة رفع بخور عشية** only when that is the actual rite/event documented.
- **الكنيسة الأم / الكنيسة التابعة** only when the historical relationship is established; avoid assumptions.

## Historical precision
1. Never upgrade a family-provided fact to an independently verified fact without evidence.
2. Distinguish **شهادة عائلية**, **شهادة مباشرة**, **مصدر صحفي معاصر**, **مصدر كنسي**, **وثيقة أولية**, **مصدر ثانوي**, and **مصدر أرشيفي**.
3. When a source is partisan, preserve the source's historical value but attribute its claims: **«بحسب المصدر...»**.
4. Do not convert allegations into facts.
5. Keep the event date separate from publication date.
6. Keep a person's role at the time of an event separate from their later role.
7. Preserve historical names of institutions while also recording current names when they differ.
8. Record conflicting dates rather than silently choosing one.

## Names and titles
Use the person's ecclesiastical title appropriate to the date being described. Example: if a source concerns the period when someone was a priest, do not retroactively style the person by a later episcopal title inside the historical event narrative unless explicitly needed for identification.

## Place terminology
Use official and historically attested forms. Record Arabic, English and historical variants as aliases. Example fields:
- canonical_name
- Arabic name
- English transliteration
- historical aliases
- governorate
- district/markaz
- village/city

## Media terminology
Use professional archival labels:
- **صورة فوتوغرافية**
- **صورة أرشيفية**
- **لقطة ثابتة من فيديو**
- **تسجيل مرئي**
- **تسجيل صوتي**
- **مقابلة مصورة**
- **مادة صحفية مصورة**
- **شهادة مسجلة**
- **نسخة معاد نشرها**
- **مصدر أصلي / نسخة أرشيفية / نسخة مشتقة**

## Writing biography and articles
The archive may contain both structured facts and long-form historical writing. Long-form articles should:
- establish chronology;
- explain ecclesiastical and local context;
- distinguish evidence from interpretation;
- link every important assertion to sources;
- use quotations sparingly and accurately;
- connect people, places and events through explicit relationships;
- avoid hagiographic language unless the piece is explicitly devotional or commemorative.

## Search and SEO language
SEO titles and descriptions should remain natural and historically accurate. Do not keyword-stuff or manufacture claims for search ranking. Prefer meaningful terms such as:
- القمص ويصا صبحي تادرس
- تاريخ إيبارشية ديرمواس ودلجا
- الأنبا أغابيوس
- الأنبا بقطر
- ديرمواس
- دلجا
- تاريخ الكنائس القبطية في ديرمواس
- أرشيف الكنيسة القبطية الأرثوذكسية في ديرمواس

## Translation policy
Translations must preserve ecclesiastical meaning. Do not mechanically translate Coptic Orthodox titles into generic Western Christian terminology when a more accurate English equivalent or transliteration exists. Preserve original-language wording alongside translation for significant titles and quotations.

## Review checklist
Before publishing a page:
- Is the title appropriate to the date?
- Is the ecclesiastical terminology accurate?
- Are event and publication dates separated?
- Are all claims sourced?
- Are conflicting claims visible?
- Are names disambiguated?
- Are media rights/provenance recorded?
- Is the wording respectful but not promotional?
- Could a historian audit the page and reconstruct the evidence trail?
