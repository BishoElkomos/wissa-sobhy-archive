# Project North Star

## Mission
Build a rigorous, open-ended digital historical archive beginning with Father Wissa Sobhy Tadros and designed to expand into the history of Deir Mawas, Anba Agabius, Anba Boktor, the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga, churches, people, places, institutions, events, media, and documentary evidence.

## Architecture principle
The archive is a connected historical knowledge base, not a biography-only website.

### Core entities
- People
- Families
- Places
- Churches / monasteries
- Diocesan institutions
- Civil institutions
- Security institutions
- Events
- Publications / news articles
- Photographs
- Videos
- Documents
- Oral/family testimony
- External sources

### Evidence principle
Every factual claim should be traceable to one or more sources. Family-supplied information is preserved and clearly labeled rather than silently mixed with independently verified material.

### Growth path
1. Father Wissa Sobhy personal and ministry archive.
2. His family, mentors, teachers, friends and contemporaries.
3. Anba Agabius and the formative Deir Mawas era.
4. Anba Boktor and the contemporary diocesan era.
5. Deir Mawas and Delga local history.
6. Diocese-wide church, institution and event history.
7. Multilingual press and international human-rights documentation.
8. Public social-media and photographic archive.

## Editorial standard
- Preserve every useful lead.
- Deduplicate at the canonical-event/article level without destroying source traces.
- Separate independent corroboration from syndication.
- Record uncertainty and conflicting evidence explicitly.
- Avoid imputing motives or making allegations beyond what a source supports.
- Protect non-public personal information.
- Prefer durable source records and archival copies for link rot resilience.

## Current research priorities
- 2007 Deir Mawas possession/enforcement dispute and police-station detention claims.
- 2010 Camelia Shahatah crisis and multilingual coverage.
- 2013 Deir Mawas/Delga attacks, field documentation, churches, police, governorate and local administration.
- 2014 restoration and post-attack worship.
- 2015 church-security crisis involving Minya Governorate and Deir Mawas Diocese.
- Elaph remembered source.
- Human Rights Watch remembered source.
- Public Facebook pages/posts and historical photographs.
- Foreign-language coverage in English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Russian and other relevant languages.
