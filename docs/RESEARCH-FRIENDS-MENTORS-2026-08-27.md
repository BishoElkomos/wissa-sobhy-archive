# Research Update — Friends & Spiritual Mentor

## Family-supplied relationships

The family identifies the following people as long-term friends or companions of Father Wissa Sobhy:

- Father Isaac Yacoub — priest serving St. Mina / Archangel Raphael and St. Mina Church, Alf Maskan, Cairo.
- Mr. William Kyrillos — educator in Tanta.
- Father Boutros Eida — Tanta.

The family identifies Father Barsoum Henein Hanna of Tanta as a spiritual mentor for Father Wissa.

These relationship claims remain family testimony unless independently corroborated.

## Internet corroboration found in this research pass

### Father Isaac Yacoub
A 2013 Youm7 report lists Father Isaac Yacoub as serving at St. Mina Church in Alf Maskan and records his assignment to celebrate Easter in Brandon, Manitoba, Canada. This corroborates the identity/church context, but does **not** prove the claimed friendship with Father Wissa.

### Father Barsoum Henein Hanna
St-Takla has a biographical page identifying Father Barsoum Henein Hanna as serving at the St. George the Martyr church in El-Hamra, Tanta; it records priestly ordination on 14 July 1972 and elevation to hegumen on 19 November 1992.

A 2018 Youm7 interview also identifies Father Barsoum Henein as a priest serving in Tanta and gives first-person historical testimony about Anba Epiphanius and Tanta church life. This supports his public identity and long service in Tanta, but does **not** independently establish his mentorship of Father Wissa.

### Father Boutros Eida / Mr. William Kyrillos
The current search pass did not locate sufficiently reliable independent evidence that securely identifies the exact individuals and their relationship to Father Wissa. Do not promote these relationships to corroborated status yet.

## Editorial rule

The archive must preserve the distinction between:

- family testimony;
- identity/church-service corroboration;
- direct evidence of friendship;
- direct evidence of spiritual mentorship.

A source proving that a person served at a particular church does not prove that person was Father Wissa's friend or mentor.
