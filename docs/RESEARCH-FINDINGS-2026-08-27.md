# Research Findings — 27 August 2026

## Newly reviewed web evidence

### 2007 — Deir Mawas property dispute and police-station detention
A contemporaneous *Al-Masry Al-Youm* report dated 29 April 2007 states that a dispute concerning a house purchased for the bishopric involved the participation of several clergy, explicitly naming Archpriest Wissa Sobhy and Fr. Maximos Talaat among clergy present with the diocesan lawyer. The report attributes the account of the dispute to Anba Agabius. This is a high-value contemporaneous press source for the relationship between Wissa Sobhy, Anba Agabius, the diocese, local security authorities and the April 2007 incident.

Source: https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/2138588

### 2007 — Police station / clergy detention lead
A Coptic History archive page and older forum reproductions contain photographs and a repeated account alleging that clergy, including Wissa Sobhy, were held at Deir Mawas police station on 24 April 2007. These are valuable archival leads, especially because the material includes historical images, but they should not be treated as independently verified merely because multiple websites repeat the same text. The source chain must be mapped and, where possible, corroborated with contemporaneous mainstream press or official records.

Sources:
- https://www.coptichistory.org/new_page_2405.htm
- https://arabchurch.com/forums/threads/%D8%B0%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B7-%D9%83%D9%89-%D9%84%D8%A7-%D9%86%D9%86%D8%B3%D9%89.116907/page-2
- https://www.copts.net/forum/showthread.php?t=21510

### 2010 — Camelia Shehata coverage
An *Al-Masry Al-Youm* report records Wissa Sobhy, identified as deputy of the Deir Mawas bishopric, commenting on a circulated video attributed to Camelia Shehata and saying that he had heard about the recording but had not watched it and therefore could not judge whether the person shown was her. This is useful evidence of his public role and media statements during the 2010 controversy.

Source: https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/14080

A contemporaneous Copts United report dated 23 July 2010 records statements attributed to Wissa Sobhy regarding the disappearance of the wife of Fr. Tadros and demonstrations in Cairo. This should be preserved as a source record and cross-checked against other contemporary reports.

Source: https://www.copts-united.com/PrintPage.php?A=20644

### 2013 — Deir Mawas and Delga field reporting
A *Watani* field report dated September 2013 describes a tour with Wissa Sobhy through Deir Mawas, documenting damage and destruction and naming specific locations and image filenames. This is especially valuable because it links the person, place, event and photographic record in one contemporaneous report.

Source: https://www.wataninet.com/2013/09/%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%B1%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B3-%D8%A8%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7/

A *Mobtada* report dated 15 September 2013 contains a first-person account attributed to Wissa Sobhy concerning conditions affecting Christians in Delga and identifies the Diocese of Deir Mawas context. It includes video and photographs and should therefore be entered into the media index as well as the source index.

Source: https://www.mobtada.com/cases/102560/%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%88-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%AF%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84%D8%AA-%D9%84%D9%82%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AE%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%B6%D8%AF-%D8%A3%D9%82%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7

### 2026 — 35th anniversary coverage
A *Sada El-Balad* report dated 8 April 2026 records the Diocese's celebration of 35 years since Wissa Sobhy's priestly ordination and identifies Anba Boktor and the diocesan clergy as participants. The article also repeats biographical information about his upbringing, education and ordination. Treat repeated biographical claims as source-attributed until independently corroborated.

Source: https://www.elbalad.news/6928700

### Core ecclesiastical biography source
St-Takla's clergy page records the priestly ordination date as 11 February 1991, the initial priestly title, service at St George Church in Deir Mawas, the elevation to archpriesthood on 22 June 1997, and his role as deputy of the bishopric. This remains a useful secondary ecclesiastical reference and should be cross-linked to the family archive and primary church records when available.

Source: https://st-takla.org/characters/priest-pastor-father/waaw/weeca-subhi-tadrus.html

## Important caution
A search result or repeated secondary text is not itself independent corroboration. The 2007 police-station account in particular should remain a `research_lead` until stronger independent evidence is located. The 2010 and 2013 reports should be preserved as contemporaneous source records and linked to their media.
