# Research Findings — 27 August 2026

## Newly reviewed web evidence

### 2007 — Deir Mawas property dispute
A contemporaneous *Al-Masry Al-Youm* report dated 29 April 2007 states that a dispute concerning a house purchased for the bishopric involved several clergy and explicitly names Archpriest Wissa Sobhy and Fr. Maximos Talaat among clergy present with the diocesan lawyer. The report attributes the account of the dispute to Anba Agabius.

Source: https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/2138588

**Archive status:** high-value contemporaneous press source. It establishes that the article named Wissa Sobhy in the incident; the article's narrative and allegations remain attributed to the source.

### 2007 — Police-station detention lead
A Coptic History archive page and older forum reproductions contain photographs and a repeated account alleging that clergy, including Wissa Sobhy, were held at Deir Mawas police station on 24 April 2007. The material is historically valuable because it includes images and a detailed list of clergy, but the websites appear to reproduce/mirror older material.

Sources:
- https://www.coptichistory.org/new_page_2405.htm
- https://arabchurch.com/forums/threads/%D8%B0%D8%A7%D9%83%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B7-%D9%83%D9%89-%D9%84%D8%A7-%D9%86%D9%86%D8%B3%D9%89.116907/page-2
- https://www.copts.net/forum/showthread.php?t=21510

**Archive status:** `research_lead`; do not count the reproductions as independent corroboration until the original report or independent contemporary evidence is recovered.

### 2010 — Camelia Shehata coverage
An *Al-Masry Al-Youm* report records Wissa Sobhy, identified as deputy of the Deir Mawas bishopric, commenting on a circulated video attributed to Camelia Shehata and saying he had heard about the recording but had not watched it and therefore could not judge whether the person shown was her.

Source: https://www.almasryalyoum.com/news/details/14080

A contemporaneous Copts United report dated 23 July 2010 records statements attributed to Wissa Sobhy regarding the disappearance of the wife of Fr. Tadros and demonstrations in Cairo.

Source: https://www.copts-united.com/PrintPage.php?A=20644

**Archive status:** contemporaneous reported statements; preserve with attribution and seek original/independent corroboration where possible.

### 2010 — German-language international lead
A German-language report dated 26 July 2010 identifies Wissa Sobhy as Bishop Agabius's deputy and reproduces/characterizes statements attributed to him, including references to audiovisual material.

Source: https://akref.ead.de/akref-nachrichten/2010/juli/26072010-aegyptendie-entfuehrte-gattin-eines-koptischen-priesters-ist-zurueck/

**Archive status:** international secondary source. Its framing is strongly partisan, so it is useful primarily for source discovery, quotations/media leads and evidence of international circulation, not as neutral corroboration.

### 2013 — Delga before August 14
Human Rights Watch's 23 July 2013 report documents attacks in Delga on 3 July, including St. George's Coptic Catholic Church and al-Saleh Church, and records interviews with a priest and a Coptic lawyer.

Source: https://www.hrw.org/news/2013/07/23/egypt-sectarian-attacks-amid-political-crisis

**Archive status:** high-value independent human-rights documentation for the event context; it does not establish every claim concerning Wissa Sobhy personally.

### 2013 — Mass attacks and Dalga/Delga
Human Rights Watch's 21 August 2013 report documents widespread attacks on churches and Christian property following 14 August. It includes Dalga/Delga in Minya governorate, records interviews with witnesses, priests and Coptic activists, and says researchers visited sites and spoke with the Minya security directorate. It lists Saints Mary and Ibram Church in Dalga among attacked churches.

Source: https://www.hrw.org/news/2013/08/21/egypt-mass-attacks-churches

The report also connects the event to the wider Minya security context and records statements from the head of the Minya security directorate.

**Archive status:** high-value independent source for the wider event, location and institutional context.

### 2013 — Deir Mawas field reporting with Wissa Sobhy
A *Watani* field report dated September 2013 describes a tour with Wissa Sobhy through Deir Mawas, identifying him as deputy of the diocese and documenting damaged sites. It names specific locations and contains image filenames such as `DSCF7281.JPG`, `DSCF7294.JPG` and `IMG_0576.JPG`. The report also says he pointed out damage to his own residence near the diocese.

Source: https://www.wataninet.com/2013/09/%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%B2-%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%B1%D9%85%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%B3-%D8%A8%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%A7%D9%81%D8%B8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7/

**Archive status:** high-value contemporary press/multimedia lead. Recover the original image files and metadata before republication.

### 2013 — Delga multimedia
*Mobtada*, 15 September 2013, is explicitly a photo/video report and attributes a first-person account to Wissa Sobhy concerning conditions in Delga and attacks on churches and Christian property.

Source: https://www.mobtada.com/cases/102560/%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%81%D9%8A%D8%AF%D9%8A%D9%88-%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%B1%D8%AF%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%A7-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%84%D8%AA-%D9%84%D9%82%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AE%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%B6%D8%AF-%D8%A3%D9%82%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A7

**Archive status:** contemporary multimedia source; media provenance and original video/image files should be recovered.

### 2013 — HRW report as a source for the institutional graph
HRW's August 2013 documentation is particularly useful for connecting **Delga → Minya Governorate → security directorate → churches → clergy/witnesses → attacks**, even when Wissa Sobhy is not personally named. This should become a separate event/institutional context record rather than being forced into his personal biography.

### 2026 — 35th anniversary
A *Sada El-Balad* report dated 8 April 2026 records the Diocese's celebration of 35 years since Wissa Sobhy's priestly ordination and identifies Anba Boktor and diocesan clergy as participants.

Source: https://www.elbalad.news/6928700

**Archive status:** contemporary institutional/news source; repeated biographical claims should remain source-attributed until independently corroborated.

### Core ecclesiastical biography source
St-Takla records the priestly ordination date as 11 February 1991, service at St George Church in Deir Mawas, elevation to archpriesthood on 22 June 1997, and his role as deputy of the bishopric.

Source: https://st-takla.org/characters/priest-pastor-father/waaw/weeca-subhi-tadrus.html

**Archive status:** useful secondary ecclesiastical reference; cross-link to primary church/family records when available.

## 2015 — Minya Governorate lead remains unresolved
Fresh searches on Arabic and English variants did **not** locate a reliable 2015 article naming Wissa Sobhy together with the Minya governor. Results were dominated by unrelated people with similar names and obituary notices.

**Decision:** the previously remembered 2015 governor/Wissa item remains an open `research_lead`. Do not publish the relationship as verified until the original article, archive capture or reliable independent copy is recovered.
