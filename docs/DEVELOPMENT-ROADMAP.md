# Development Roadmap

## North Star
Deliver a world-class bilingual digital historical archive whose permanent principal identity is **Archpriest Wissa Sobhy Tadrous**, with a scalable knowledge architecture for the wider history of Deir Mawas, the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga, its clergy, churches, institutions and related figures.

- [x] Establish media provenance policy.
- [x] Establish Arabic/English-first internationalization architecture.

- [x] Establish legal/policy document set.

## Quality gates
No public historical page should ship unless it passes:
1. Identity/disambiguation review.
2. Chronology review.
3. Source/provenance review.
4. Ecclesiastical terminology review.
5. Arabic/English editorial review.
6. Media rights/provenance review.
7. SEO/accessibility review.
8. Mobile/desktop usability review.

## Speed principle
Move quickly, but never trade away provenance. The fastest acceptable path is **structured iteration with auditable commits**, not publishing unverified claims.
