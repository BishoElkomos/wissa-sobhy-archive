## Family archive intake
For each supplied item, record:
- archive ID
- original filename
- approximate date
- exact date if known
- people visible / named
- place
- occasion
- photographer / source if known
- physical original available (yes/no)
- scan quality
- publication permission
- notes

## Research rule
A lead is not a fact. A copied article is not independent corroboration. Preserve the original wording and source metadata before writing a normalized historical claim.
