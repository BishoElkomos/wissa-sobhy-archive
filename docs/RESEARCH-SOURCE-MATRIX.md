## Priority levels
### A — Primary recovery
Original church records, certificates, letters, photographs, contemporaneous newspaper originals, recordings and official documents.

### B — Strong contemporaneous evidence
Contemporaneous reputable press, independent human-rights documentation and direct interviews.

### C — Secondary corroboration
Ecclesiastical directories, later articles, scholarly publications and reputable summaries.

### D — Discovery leads
Search snippets, reposts, forums, unsourced social comments and derivative pages.

## Important rule
A source can be highly valuable for context without proving a personal claim about Wissa Sobhy. Every claim must be linked to the evidence that actually supports it.
