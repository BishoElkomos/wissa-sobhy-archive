# Research Note — Father Wissa Sobhy Birth Date

## Current archival fact

The family has confirmed that Father Wissa Sobhy's date of birth is **4 December 1956**.

This should be treated as a family-confirmed fact for the archive's internal research record. It must still be distinguished from the date of priestly ordination (**11 February 1991**) and the date of elevation to hegumen (**22 June 1997**).

## Public-source cross-check

A secondary web page currently states: "مواليد 4 ديسمبر 1956 في طنطا" and attributes its biographical footnotes to St-Takla and 2026 coverage. Because this page is derivative, it is not sufficient by itself as the primary evidence.

## Publication policy

When the public biography is prepared, the birth date may be displayed as a verified archival fact based on family confirmation, while the archive should continue seeking an independent primary or institutional record if one can be obtained.
