Profile

The official Coptic Orthodox Church record identifies Bishop Boktor as the current bishop of the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga and records his episcopal ordination on 1 June 2025. Secondary reference material provides additional biographical details, but this archive distinguishes those published details from information independently confirmed by primary records.

Archival rule: disputed or non-primary biographical details are not presented as settled facts until independently reconciled.

Diocesan succession

The diocese was established on 13 November 1988 with the episcopal ordination of Agabius. Following Metropolitan Agabius's repose on 4 February 2025, Bishop Boktor was ordained for the diocese on 1 June 2025. This sequence forms a central part of the archive's modern diocesan timeline.

Documented milestones

1 Jun 2025

Episcopal ordination

Bishop Boktor was ordained Bishop of Deir Mawas and Delga.

Jun 2025

Enthronement

His enthronement was held in Deir Mawas in the presence of church, official and public representatives.

Mar 2026

Two priestly ordinations

The first priestly ordinations reported for his episcopate were held at the Virgin Mary Church in Deir Mawas.

13 Jun 2026

St Abram celebrations at Delga

The bishop celebrated the feast liturgy; five consecrated women were received and 35 members of the diocese were ordained in the Psaltes rank.

27 Jul 2026

Meeting with Pope Tawadros II

He was received at the Papal Residence in Cairo regarding pastoral matters concerning the diocese.
