Life and ministry

Bishop Boktor was born on 5 February 1975 in the village of Nazlet Hanna, in the El-Fashn district of Beni Suef Governorate, and grew up serving at St George Church in his village before turning to monastic life. The early-life details below rest on Bishop Boktor's profile in the St-Takla bishops register, and the official Coptic Orthodox Church bishops record confirms his dates of birth and monasticism.

5 Feb 1975

Birth and upbringing

Born in Nazlet Hanna, El-Fashn, Beni Suef; before monasticism he served at St George Church in his village as a preparatory-class servant and servants-meeting secretary.

Education

University and theological study

He earned a Bachelor of Arts and Education (Social Studies), then a Bachelor of Theological Sciences in Alexandria in 2017, and is a master's researcher at Holy Sophia University (USA) on "Monastic Roots in the Holy Bible".

23 Feb 2007

Monastic life - Father Daniel El-Gourgy

He became a monk at St George Monastery, El-Khatatba (El-Sadat, Monufia), receiving the monastic name Daniel El-Gourgy.

15 Feb 2010

Priestly ordination

He was ordained a priest on 15 February 2010.

13 Aug 2012

Hegumen

He was elevated to the rank of hegumen on 13 August 2012.

Published material on his early life and pre-episcopal years remains limited so far; the archive welcomes any document or trusted testimony that enriches that period of his story.

Diocesan succession

The diocese was established on 13 November 1988 with the episcopal ordination of Agabius. Following Metropolitan Agabius's repose on 4 February 2025, Bishop Boktor was ordained for the diocese on 1 June 2025. This sequence forms a central part of the archive's modern diocesan timeline.

Documented milestones

1 Jun 2025

Episcopal ordination

Bishop Boktor was ordained Bishop of Deir Mawas and Delga.

19 Jun 2025

Enthronement

His enthronement was held in Deir Mawas on the evening of Thursday 19 June 2025 in the presence of church, official and public representatives, and completed with a liturgy the following morning.

30 Jun 2025

First clergy assembly and pastoral tours

He chaired the first assembly of the diocese's priests and began pastoral tours among the villages.

2 Aug 2025

Six hegumens ordained and a consecration

He elevated six priests of the diocese to the rank of hegumen and consecrated a new deaconess, the first ordinations of his episcopate.

1 Mar 2026

First priestly ordinations of his episcopate

He ordained two deacons as priests during a liturgy at the Virgin Mary Church in Deir Mawas, the first priestly ordinations after his enthronement.

13 Jun 2026

St Abram celebrations at Delga

The bishop celebrated the feast liturgy; five consecrated women were received and 35 members of the diocese were ordained in the Psaltes rank.

27 Jul 2026

Meeting with Pope Tawadros II

He was received at the Papal Residence in Cairo regarding pastoral matters concerning the diocese.
