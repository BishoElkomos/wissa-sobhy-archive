HG Bishop Boktor

Bishop of the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga since 1 June 2025

Successor of the late Metropolitan Agabius on the see of Deir Mawas and Delga, leading the diocese in its current phase with continuing pastoral and institutional service in its cities and villages.

Biography and service

Born on 5 February 1975 in the village of Nazlet Hanna, El-Fashn district, Beni Suef Governorate, he grew up serving at St George Church in his village as a preparatory-stage servant and leader of the servants' meeting. He earned a BA in Arts and Education (Social Studies), then a Bachelor of Theological Sciences in Alexandria in 2017, and is a master's researcher on "Monastic roots in the Holy Scripture."

He turned to monastic life at St George Monastery, El-Khatatba, under the name Monk Fr Daniel El-Gergawy, was ordained priest on 15 February 2010, and received the hegumen rank on 13 August 2012.

The transition of the diocese's leadership

The Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga was tied to Metropolitan Agabius from his ordination on 13 November 1988. After his repose on 4 February 2025, Bishop Boktor was ordained bishop over it by the laying on of hands of His Holiness Pope Tawadros II on 1 June 2025, and was enthroned on its see at the Church of the Virgin Mary, Deir Mawas, on 19 June 2025, with Metropolitan Demetrius of Mallawi, the patriarchal deputy over the diocese, presiding over the enthronement prayers.
