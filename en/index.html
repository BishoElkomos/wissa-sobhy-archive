Research principles

  • Primary and institutional records are preferred when available.
  • Published reports are used with their date, outlet and scope recorded.
  • Family-held photographs and documents are identified as archival material rather than presented as independent published sources.
  • Conflicting dates are flagged instead of silently reconciled.
