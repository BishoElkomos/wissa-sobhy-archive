ARCHIVE METHOD

How research becomes an archive

Person→Role→Institution→Place→Event→Source→Media

Each new finding should become a durable record: a person, relationship, place, event, source, document, photograph, video or research lead. Conflicting accounts are preserved rather than silently erased.
