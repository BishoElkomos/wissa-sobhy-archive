The archive's methodology

About this archive

This archive is a historical documentation project gathering what has been documented of the life of Fr Wissa Sobhy Ibrahim Tadros and of the history of the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga and its bishops, and every newly documented addition joins it.

How does the archive document its materials?

The site's statements are tied to their origins on the References page: the report with its date, outlet and link, the photograph with its source and occasion, the recording with its publisher's channel. What comes from the family archive is expressly attributed to it, and the family's testimony about its own history is written in the body directly as the testimony of its owners. What is not established by a source is not displayed.

Sources and news

Title, outlet, date and link

Every source's details are kept in full, and a repost is not counted as an independent source without being marked as such.

Photos and recordings

Clear attribution for every item

Photos are attributed to their occasions and their known subjects, and recordings to their publishers' channels under their published titles.

The family archive

Family and church holdings

Family materials are classified as archival holdings and expressly attributed, and are not treated as press sources.

One rule does not change

The source before the conclusion. What is not established by a source is not displayed, and whatever a biography lacks is completed by research and documentation, never by guessing.

Have a photo, a document, or a correction?

The archive welcomes every trustworthy item that enriches this memory or corrects it.
