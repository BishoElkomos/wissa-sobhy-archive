The Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga is an essential institutional context for understanding Father Wissa Sobhy Tadros, Metropolitan Agabius and the events documented in this archive.

The archive records the diocese as an independent historical subject rather than treating its history as a footnote to an individual biography.
