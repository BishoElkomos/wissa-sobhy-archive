Archival method

Published evidence

News reports and reference sources are preserved with their outlet, date, link and reliability status where available.

Media provenance

Media records distinguish published material from links whose files or video pages have not yet been independently inspected.

Original holdings

Family and church records remain classified as archival holdings rather than public independent publications.

The page reads the canonical source registry and media registry directly. No record is removed merely because it is pending verification.
