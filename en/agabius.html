The late Met. Agabius

Bishop then Metropolitan of Deir Mawas and Delga • 1944–2025

From Zagazig to the wilderness of Scetis, from the stewardship of St Bishoy's Monastery to the episcopate of Deir Mawas and Delga: thirty-six years of building and pastoral care, in which Fr Wissa Sobhy was discipled at his hands and served as his deputy.

From Zagazig to the wilderness of Scetis

He was born Michel Nassef Abdallah in Zagazig on 2 April 1944, and his family moved to Tanta, where he was attached to the Church of the Virgin Mary at Al-Sagha and was discipled under Fr Hanna Bastawros. He graduated from the Faculty of Agriculture, Cairo University, in 1968, worked as an engineer in agricultural land reclamation, and completed his military service until 1974 with the rank of captain engineer.

He headed to the wilderness of Scetis on 12 July 1977 and took the monastic habit at the Monastery of St Bishoy under the name Monk Wissa Anba Bishoy on 15 December 1977 at the hands of Pope Shenouda III; he was ordained priest on 17 July 1979, then hegumen and steward of the monastery on 1 January 1981. He oversaw the preparation of the holy Myron and was among Pope Shenouda's close aides during the years of confinement.

Bishop of Deir Mawas and Delga

Pope Shenouda III chose him for the episcopate of Deir Mawas and Delga after the Kasr Heshmat crisis; he was ordained bishop on 13 November 1988 and enthroned on the see of the diocese on 5 August 1989. Over more than three decades he led a wide building movement: new churches and rebuilt ones, the church documentation offices since 1991, the house of consecration and the consecrated virgins at Delga, the services building, and the Agapy Farm project.

On 20 November 2022 he was elevated to the rank of metropolitan. He departed in the Lord on 4 February 2025, and funeral prayers were held at St Mark's Cathedral, Abbasiya, then at Deir Mawas.

Metropolitan Agabius and Fr Wissa

His spiritual son, whom he ordained and who served with him until his repose

From the time the young Samir Sobhy moved to Deir Mawas in 1988, Metropolitan Agabius gave this disciple a special place: he prepared him for the priesthood, laid hands on him as priest on 11 February 1991, raised him to the hegumenate on 22 June 1997 and entrusted him with the deputyship of the Metropolis. Twenty-eight years of shared work in pastoral care, documentation and visitation across the cities and villages of the diocese, until the Metropolitan's repose in February 2025.
