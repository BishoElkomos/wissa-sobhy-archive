How to read the evidence

  • High confidence means the archive has strong published or institutional support.
  • Documented means the claim is supported by a specific archival record or source.
  • Research lead means the material is useful for further investigation but should not be treated as established fact.
  • Conflicting dates should be flagged and traced to their sources rather than silently harmonized.
