Fr Wissa Sobhy Tadros

Hegumen of the Church of the Virgin Mary, Deir Mawas — priest since 11 February 1991

Born in Tanta in 1956, he was discipled by Metropolitan Agabius and moved with him to Deir Mawas, where Agabius ordained him priest in 1991 and raised him to hegumen in 1997; he then served as deputy of the Metropolis of Deir Mawas and Delga for twenty-eight years. More than 35 years of priesthood, and church service that began in his youth.

From Tanta to Deir Mawas

Fr Wissa Sobhy Tadros — born Samir Sobhy Ibrahim Tadros — was born on 4 December 1956 in Al-Hekma district, Tanta, and grew up at St Michael the Archangel Church under Fr Surial Abdu. He completed his secondary education in Tanta, graduated from the Faculty of Commerce, Tanta University, in 1980, and from his youth was devoted to service, Sunday-school teaching, visitation and retreats.

In 1988 — the year Metropolitan Agabius was ordained bishop of Deir Mawas and Delga — he moved to Deir Mawas to serve with his spiritual father and teacher, in preparation for priestly ordination.

Ordination and Hegumenate

On 11 February 1991 he was ordained priest under the name Fr Wissa Sobhy, by the laying on of hands of Metropolitan Agabius and Bishop Thomas, Metropolitan of El-Qusiya. He spent the forty days at the Monastery of the Virgin Mary at Al-Muharraq, received the altar there at the liturgy of 1 March 1991, and returned on 21 March to begin his priestly ministry in the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga.

On 22 June 1997 he was raised to the rank of hegumen, and with that began his deputyship of the Metropolis, which continued until the repose of Metropolitan Agabius in February 2025.

He married Caroline Makram Ghaly on 10 February 1991, the eve of his ordination, and she has shared the journey of service from its beginning.

His spiritual father and teacher, Metropolitan Agabius

A relationship of discipleship and service that ran from 1988 to 2025

Fr Wissa moved with his spiritual father and teacher Metropolitan Agabius from Tanta to Deir Mawas in 1988 as a disciple preparing for the priesthood; Agabius laid hands on him as priest on 11 February 1991, raised him to the hegumenate on 22 June 1997 and entrusted him with the deputyship of the Metropolis. Twenty-eight years of shared ministry in pastoral care, documentation and visitation across the cities and villages of the diocese, until Metropolitan Agabius departed in the Lord on 4 February 2025.
