Current ministry

Priest of the Church of the Virgin Mary, Deir Mawas.

The archive records his previous administrative role separately: Vicar of the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga, 1991–2025.

Basic information

  • Born: December 4, 1956 — Tanta, Egypt
  • Education: Bachelor of Commerce, Tanta University — 1980
  • Priestly ordination: February 11, 1991
  • Elevated to Hegumen: June 22, 1997
