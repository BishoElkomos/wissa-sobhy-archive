Archive of Abuna Wissa Sobhy Tadros

A Coptic Orthodox Priest (1956-)

About This Archive

This archive documents the life and ministry of Abuna Wissa Sobhy Tadros, a Coptic Orthodox priest who has served the Church for over 35 years.

Basic Information

  • Birth: December 4, 1956 - Tanta
  • Education: Bachelor of Commerce - Tanta University (1980)
  • Ordination: February 11, 1991
  • Rank: Hegumen (Abuna) since June 22, 1997
  • Position: Vicar of the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga
