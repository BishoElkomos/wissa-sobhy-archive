Archival method

Sources & news

Title, outlet, date, link and status are preserved where available. Syndicated or reproduced material is not silently counted as independent evidence.

Photographs & video

Supplied or uninspected media remains under review. Dates, occasions and identities are not inferred from filenames alone.

Internal holdings

Family and church-held material is classified as an archival holding, not automatically as a public journalistic source.

Rule: the integration is in presentation and navigation, not deletion or replacement. Data is read from source-registry.json, media-registry.json and photo-registry.json.
