Documented Biography

Father Wissa Sobhy Tadros

Priest of the Church of the Virgin Mary, Deir Mawas

Identity and current ministry

Father Wissa Sobhy Tadros is an Egyptian Coptic Orthodox priest whose documented ministry is closely connected with Deir Mawas and its diocese.

Current position: Priest of the Church of the Virgin Mary, Deir Mawas.
Previous administrative position: Vicar of the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga, 1991–2025. The archive treats this as a former position, not his current title.

Early life and education

He was born on December 4, 1956 in Tanta, Egypt. He completed a Bachelor of Commerce at Tanta University in 1980.

Beginnings of service

Before priestly ordination, he was active in church service in Tanta, including service at the Church of Archangel Michael. The archive preserves this period as part of the formation of his later pastoral ministry.

Connection with Deir Mawas

The archive records his move into the Deir Mawas context in 1988. This date is treated as an important transition in his connection with the diocese and is not used to imply that his later vicarial office began that year.

Priestly ordination

On February 11, 1991, he was ordained a priest by the late Metropolitan Agabius of Deir Mawas and Metropolitan Thomas of Qussia. The 2026 diocesan celebration marked 35 years since his priestly ordination.

Elevation to hegumen

On June 22, 1997, he was elevated to the rank of hegumen. The archive records this as a major milestone in his priestly service.

Ministry, responsibility and historical context

His ministry spans periods of ordinary parish service as well as moments of serious historical pressure on churches and Christian communities in Deir Mawas and Delga. The archive documents these events separately so that contemporary reporting, eyewitness material, photographs and later recollections are not collapsed into a single narrative.

The 2013 attacks and documentation

Reports from 2013 document damage affecting churches and Christian properties in Deir Mawas and Delga. The archive treats the period as an important historical chapter and links it to published reports and surviving media rather than presenting unsupported claims.

The 35th priestly jubilee

In April 2026, the Diocese of Deir Mawas and Delga marked 35 years since Father Wissa's priestly ordination. The published report identifies Metropolitan Agabius and Metropolitan Thomas as the fathers who ordained him in 1991 and records the jubilee celebration under the leadership of Metropolitan Boktor.

Relationship with Metropolitan Agabius

Metropolitan Agabius is a central figure in Father Wissa's documented priestly history because he participated in his 1991 ordination and served as metropolitan of Deir Mawas and Delga until his repose in 2025. The archive therefore maintains a separate biography for Agabius to avoid confusing the two men, especially because Agabius himself had carried the monastic name Wissa Anba Bishoy before his episcopate.

Research note

This biography distinguishes published evidence, institutional records, family-held archival material and research leads. Where a date or claim requires further verification, it should remain marked as such rather than being silently converted into certainty.
